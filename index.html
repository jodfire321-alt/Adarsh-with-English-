Spoken English Syllabus

A structured learning path from Beginner to Advanced.

01 — English Basics

Alphabet, basic words, common expressions and simple sentences.

02 — Self Introduction

Name, hobbies, family, education and personal introduction.

03 — Daily Conversation

Morning routine, school, friends, shopping, travelling and common situations.

04 — Basic Grammar

Nouns, pronouns, verbs, adjectives, articles, prepositions and conjunctions.

05 — Tenses

Present, Past and Future tense with practical speaking examples.

06 — Vocabulary Building

Daily-use words, synonyms, antonyms, phrases and useful expressions.

07 — Question & Answer

Learn WH questions, Yes/No questions and natural responses.

08 — Confidence Building

Think in English, reduce hesitation and practice speaking confidently.

09 — Advanced Conversation

Long conversations, opinions, discussions and real-world communication.

10 — Final Speaking Practice

Presentation, interview practice and complete conversation sessions.
